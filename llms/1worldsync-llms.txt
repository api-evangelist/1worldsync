# 1WorldSync

> 1WorldSync is the leading product content network and a GS1-certified GDSN data pool. Its Content1 platform exposes HMAC-authenticated REST APIs for product master data — search, fetch, count, and hierarchy retrieval — serving CPG, grocery, healthcare, foodservice, and retail. SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified.

## APIs

- [Content1 Read API 2.0](https://developer.1worldsync.com/read-api-2-0-documentation/): Search and fetch product master data, product counts, and item hierarchies (base: https://content1-api.1worldsync.com).
- [Item Management Inbound API](https://developer.1worldsync.com/inbound-api-documentation/): Add, update, delete, and link items and hierarchies.
- [Digital Asset Management API](https://developer.1worldsync.com/digital-asset-api-documentation/): Manage images and rich media.
- [User Management API (UMA)](https://developer.1worldsync.com/uma-api-documentation/): Read/write user management.
